Aina, Fresh, Mea'ai Mural

June 2025
Wahiwa Value Added Development Center

This design was a final project design when I attended Leeward Community College in 2024, two designs chosen by the Admins of the Wahiwa Value Added Center to be on the front of the building or the inside of the building.
